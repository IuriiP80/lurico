Focused diagnosis

Oven on. Food still raw.

An oven that won't reach temperature can have a failed bake or broil element, a weak igniter, a bad temperature sensor or a control problem. A technician should test it before recommending a repair.

Not heating

The oven stays cold or never reaches the set temperature.

Uneven baking

Food burns on one side and stays raw on the other.

Burner problems

A burner won't light, keeps clicking or stays on low.

Controls

Error codes, a blank display or buttons that don't respond.

!
Smell gas? Get out first.

Don't flip switches or light anything. Get everyone outside and call your gas utility or 911 from outside. Call us once the leak is handled.
