Focused diagnosis

Running, but not drying.

A dryer that tumbles but stays cool or damp can have a failed heating part, a blown thermal fuse, a faulty thermostat or a blocked exhaust vent. A technician should test it before recommending a repair.

No heat

The drum turns, but the air stays cold or barely warm.

Takes too long

Loads need two or three cycles to come out dry.

Loud drum

Squeaking, thumping or scraping while the drum turns.

Won't start

No tumble at all, or the dryer stops partway through a cycle.

!
Burning smell? Stop using the dryer.

Unplug it and call us at 954-305-5539. Lint buildup and overheating parts are a fire risk, so the dryer should be checked before the next load.
