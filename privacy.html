4. Cookies, advertising and call tracking

We use Google Ads to show our ads and measure their results. Our website loads the Google tag (gtag.js), which uses cookies to record visits and conversions, such as a submitted request or a call from our website.

Enhanced conversions: when you submit a request, the phone number you entered is passed to the Google tag so Google can match the conversion to an ad click. Google receives it in hashed form. It is used only to measure ad performance.

Call tracking: on some pages, Google may show a Google forwarding number in place of our number. Calls to that number are routed to us, and Google records call details such as call start time, duration and the caller’s area code to report ad performance.

You can control ad personalization at adssettings.google.com, learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some site features may not work without cookies.
