Focused diagnosis

Water left at the bottom.

Standing water usually points to a clogged filter or drain hose, a failed drain pump or a check-valve problem. A technician should test the dishwasher before recommending a repair.

Won't drain

Dirty dishes

Leaking

Won't start

Lights on but no cycle, or no power at all.
