Focused diagnosis

Lights on. No coffee.

A built-in coffee system that powers on but won't brew often has a blocked brew unit, scale buildup, a stuck grinder, a weak pump or a water supply problem. A technician should test it before recommending a repair.

Won't brew

It grinds or hums, but no coffee comes out.

Leaking

Water in the drip tray area or in the cabinet below.

Grinder

Loud, jammed or not grinding the beans.

Milk system

No froth, weak steam or milk leaking.

!
Water in the cabinet? Shut off the supply.

If your machine is plumbed in, close its water valve, switch the machine off, and call us at 954-305-5539. Don't keep running cycles with a leak.
