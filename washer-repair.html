Focused diagnosis

Full of water. Won't spin.

A washer that stops with water inside can have a clogged drain pump or filter, a failed door lock or lid switch, a worn belt or a control problem. A technician should test it before recommending a repair.

Won't drain

Water stays in the drum after the cycle ends.

Won't spin

Clothes come out soaking wet.

Leaking

Water on the floor during or after a cycle.

Shakes or bangs

Loud thumping or walking during the spin.
